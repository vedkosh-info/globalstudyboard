# Phase 1 — 100 Hyper-Real Image Prompts (Google Gemini)

Paste-and-go. Each prompt below is **self-contained** — copy the whole fenced block, paste into the Gemini app, generate, and save the result to the exact **path** shown. Read [`README.md`](./README.md) for the folder architecture, safety doctrine and WebP optimisation recipe. A machine-readable version of all 100 (path + alt + full prompt) is in [`manifest.json`](./manifest.json).

> Every prompt already has the shared **hyper-real style tail** appended (real camera/lens/light + anti-AI negatives + safety guardrails + palette + 16:9). Do not edit the tail between images — regenerate instead, so all 100 stay one coherent photographic set.

---

## How to generate (Gemini tips)

- Set the output ratio to 16:9 using the Gemini image aspect-ratio control (or state '16:9 widescreen' in the prompt); don't crop a square afterward — compose wide from the start.
- Generate ONE image at a time with the full identical styleTail appended, so every asset shares the same lens, light and grade; never paraphrase the tail between runs — copy-paste it verbatim for a consistent house look.
- If a face, human figure, text, logo, crest, flag, or religious/political symbol leaks in, do NOT edit — just regenerate; these artifacts recur, so re-roll 2–3 times and keep the cleanest empty frame.
- Reinforce the specific optics in the subject line, not just the tail: name the '50mm prime, f/2.8, shallow natural depth of field, soft window light from the left' — concrete camera/lighting language pushes Gemini off its default illustrative render.
- Front-load the anti-AI negatives ('a real photograph, NOT an illustration or 3D render, no plastic surfaces, no over-smoothing') near the top of the prompt as well as in the tail — early tokens carry more weight.
- Keep scenes calm and near-empty (a few archetype objects on a clean surface); busy scenes are where Gemini warps geometry, duplicates objects, and invents fake signage — simplicity reads as more real.
- After you get a clean frame, use Gemini's upscale/high-resolution option once, then inspect at 100% for melted edges, duplicated items, or hidden lettering before accepting.
- Ask for 'fine natural film grain and a subtle lens vignette' explicitly — a touch of grain and honest imperfection is the single biggest tell that separates a real photo from a too-clean AI image.

**Color grade:** Warm natural editorial grade — creamy highlights, soft stone-grey neutrals, deep forest-green and muted-navy anchors with occasional subtle gold accents; gentle contrast, true-to-life saturation, no HDR punch.

**Anti-AI negatives baked into every prompt:** not an illustration · not a 3D render · not CGI · not a painting · not digital art · no cartoon or anime · over-saturation · plastic surfaces · waxy skin or materials · over-smoothing · HDR halos · fake or exaggerated bokeh · warped or melted geometry · duplicated or cloned objects · extra or malformed limbs · watermark · text artifacts · gibberish lettering · logos or crests · people or faces · religious symbols · flags or borders · maps · weapons · harsh neon colors · banding in gradients · blurry mush · uncanny fake lighting

## Realism audit (independent scoring, 1–5)

These scores predict how a *careful* Gemini generation will look — they flag which scenes need extra re-rolls, not defects in the prompt. **Landscapes, textures and simple still-lifes are essentially guaranteed to look real; scenes with screens, printed sheets, or human-adjacent props are the hardest and need re-rolls.**

- **Average realism: 3.76 / 5** — distribution: 5★ ×7 · 4★ ×62 · 3★ ×31. No prompt scored below 3. Safest (5★): #8, #11, #17, #20, #70, #95, #98.
- **Safety: 0 high, 11 medium, 68 low (inherent/acceptable), 21 none.** "Low" just means a scene *could* leak a stray mark; the blank-surface wording handles it.
