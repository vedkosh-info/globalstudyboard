# Application Check — published routes, 6 November 2026

This note records routes added to existing Application Check programmes. Each route is a sentence read on the linked official page on 6 November 2026. A route is a description the page gives. It is not an equivalence decision, a fee, or an offer of admission.

Pages that returned an automated block on this network were not given new sentences: University of Melbourne entry requirements and University of Oxford graduate course page. Their existing 4 November checks are unchanged.

## University of British Columbia — Master of Data Science, Vancouver

Read `https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/admissions`, `https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/admissions/prerequisites` and `https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/admissions/international-students`.

- Prerequisites can be taken at UBC or an academic equivalent post-secondary institution. Another university’s course needs significant overlap with the listed objectives, not a match of every objective. The transfer-credit search is described as reference only.
- Distance courses from accredited universities are accepted when they are credit-bearing, graded and on an official transcript. MOOCs, including Coursera, and adult or continuing-studies courses do not meet the prerequisite. Those online courses can be described as refreshers only when the prerequisites are already met.
- One or two unfinished courses: the page says an interim mark or an instructor letter is typically requested. Future enrolment needs proof of enrolment. Prerequisites must be finished before September.
- A three-year degree is referred to `https://www.grad.ubc.ca/countries`. That page was not re-read country by country.
- Lower grades may be explained with work, professional experience, refresher courses or personal challenges. That explanation is not an offer. Other formal training is not part of that explanation. It sits in the exceptions paragraph and needs the Program Director’s recommendation and the Dean’s approval.
- Applicants who do not meet the stated requirements may be considered for other significant formal training, relevant professional experience or demonstrable knowledge, on the Program Director’s recommendation and the Dean’s approval.
- Vancouver English, when the last degree’s language of instruction was not English: an English test from the past 24 months. IELTS Academic 7.0 overall and 6.5 in each component is the Vancouver column. TOEFL and Duolingo are also in that column. Their numbers are not copied here. The before/after 21 January 2026 TOEFL split is in the Okanagan column and is not applied to Vancouver.

The September 2027 application deadline on the admissions page remains 31 January 2027.

## Indian Institute of Management Bangalore — EPGP

Read `https://www.iimb.ac.in/admissions/epgp-admissions/admission-process`.

- Bachelor’s degree in any discipline: at least 50% marks or an equivalent CGPA. The same page states 45% or an equivalent CGPA for SC, ST and persons-with-disability applicants. No conversion table is printed there.
- A GMAT or GRE taken at home is not accepted for EPGP 2027. The official scorecard, or the test-taker copy if the official scorecard has not arrived, must be included. The official report is used for verification.
- Work after the bachelor’s degree: at least five years as on 31 March 2027, and preferably fewer than 12 years. The preference is not encoded as a second minimum.

## Nazarbayev University — Master of Engineering Management

Read `https://nu.edu.kz/admissions/how-to-apply/masters/regular-admissions-masters/`.

Applicants are invited to take ALEKS on a set date. A valid GRE or GMAT certificate may be used to request an exemption. A request is not an exemption.
